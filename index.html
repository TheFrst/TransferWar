How it works: everyone gets the same budget. Players come up one at a time and you have a few seconds to outbid each other. Every manager needs 11 players: 1 GK, 4 DEF, 3 MID, 3 FWD. Watch for 🎁 mystery players, and use your ⏰ time-out and 🕵️ scout once per auction. Then the league fills up with AI clubs and a 14-matchday season plus a cup are played out.
